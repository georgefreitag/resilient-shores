Tigger lived contentedly in Troy Beach. He loved sunshine, prowling, and harvesting his needs from “The Humans” living along the shore. Every morning he explored and exploited all of his marking opportunities. Safety was never a concern. part2: >- Climate change brought stronger storms, higher flooding, and hotter days. Troy Beach was changing. “The Humans” began leaving. Empty homes replaced familiar faces. Handouts became scarce. Predators emerged. For seasons, Tigger waited for life to return to normal, but they never did. The fish were fewer, the shore was quieter, and the easy life disappeared. For the first time, the Cat Who Walks by Himself discovered that all places were not alike. part3: >- Up until this point, Tigger believed independence meant never needing anyone. Then all things changed. Tigger sought out “The Humans” and proposed an arrangement. If he could make the children laugh, they would provide food. If he could keep mice away, they would provide shelter during storms. Neither side mentioned ownership. Both accepted the bargain.
…The End epilogue: >- Tigger kept his promise. He made the family laugh and hunted mice. He proved he could adapt without surrendering his independence. When conditions were pleasant, he wandered outside and walked by himself. When storms arrived, he accepted shelter. When food was scarce, he accepted help. He learned that resilience did not require isolation. The Cat Who Walks by Himself remained free. He had simply learned that all places are not alike, and that wisdom sometimes means knowing when to negotiate. arc: top1: "Values" top2: "Threatened" curve_top: "Conflict" curve_bottom1: "+/- Actions" curve_bottom2: "" bottom1: "Values" bottom2: "preserved" ---
