Why create a story?

Every resilience story moves through three stages. Together they help you see where you are, what’s at stake, and where you could go next.

Context

Build shared understanding

Stories can help communities identify common ground, resolve internal conflicts, and build trust.

Conflict

Explore conflict and options for the future

Stories can provide an emotionally secure and safe space to explore recovery options, reclaim values threatened by potential changes, or rebuild toward a new desired state.

Resolutions

Create, tell, and retell stories

Stories can be personal musings or spur action for a better future.
