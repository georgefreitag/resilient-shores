Redirect your future through storytelling.

Stories can also offer insight and help redirect your future.

Start your story Read Resilient Shores

We all create and tell stories. We tell different stories for different occasions and for different reasons.

When we meet someone for the first time, we tell our story as we listen to theirs. We key our story to our audience’s interests and, in the process, better understand ourselves and the places we’ve experienced. Stories can also offer insight and help redirect your future.

Storytelling is critical to negotiating our lives. Creating and telling stories can also help frame and direct our future. This may be especially true when facing decisions that are often impossibly difficult to confront any other way.

Our climate is changing. Our world is rapidly becoming baffling and, for many of us, surprisingly foreign.

We will have to make difficult, often irreversible decisions with extreme consequences: changing careers, having a family, and relocating our residences.

Story creation and telling may offer a safe place to develop resolutions, where you can form strategies without lasting adverse consequences.

To help you address our changing world, we invite you to read Resilient Shores: Adapting to a Changing Climate Through Storytelling.

And storytelling can be innovative, restorative, and fun when engaging friends and colleagues.
